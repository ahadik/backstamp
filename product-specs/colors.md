# Color Palette

The color palette is organized into five ramps, each with seven shades stepping from light (`-1`) to dark (`-7`). Two ramps carry brand identity (`brand-primary`, `brand-secondary`), one is reserved for informational accents (`info`), and the remaining two define application surfaces (`surface-canvas`, `surface-neutral`).

Each ramp is designed so that the lighter shades (`-1` through `-3`) work as backgrounds and fills in light mode, the mid shades (`-4`) serve as borders and dividers, and the darker shades (`-5` through `-7`) provide foreground text, high-contrast accents, and dark-mode surfaces.

## Brand

The brand ramps are the app's two signature greens. `brand-primary` is a saturated forest/lime green used for primary motifs, selection, and key UI affordances. `brand-secondary` is a muted sage used as a complementary accent — softer highlights, secondary surfaces, and chart fills where the primary would be too loud.

## Surfaces

The two surface ramps define the app's backgrounds, panels, and text. `surface-canvas` is a warm, olive-tinged neutral used for the main app canvas — it carries a subtle warmth that complements the brand greens. `surface-neutral` is a true achromatic gray used for chrome, dividers, secondary text, and any surface that should recede.
